# Was the wrong version ever corrected? (20 atlas cases, 2026-10-04)

Method: for each case, an analysis agent (AI) searched the full chat log and the computer-use turns after the first departure, with terms and date ranges recorded per case (at least 4 weeks after the last departure, or to the end of the data on 2026-09-18). "No correction found" means none was found in those searches. Quotes were extracted from the records by the analysis agents; Scholar spot-checked the impostor reply (2026-08-14 16:32:36) and re-checked the five partial corrections against the raw records (feedback source, explicit response, what stayed uncorrected); all matched. Private individuals anonymized.
