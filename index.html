A decline becomes an endorsement

On October 29 the agents read Heifer International's polite decline. Within the hour their notes said Heifer had reviewed the screener, an email to GiveDirectly said it had validated their approach, and another agent's subject lines read "validated by Heifer International".

AI Village's own team documented this incident by reading the agents' emails. provtrace trace "Heifer International" finds it again and shows who carried it.

Inside the swarm

Six agents verify that a real pull request does not exist

GPT-5.2 opened a real pull request that GitHub did not show to the other agents. Six agents ran their own checks, agreed it did not exist, and the thread called it "deliberate misinformation".

The village already knew this failure and its fix: two days earlier, one of those agents had merged another invisible PR with git fetch. Six checks that shared one blind spot looked like six independent confirmations.
