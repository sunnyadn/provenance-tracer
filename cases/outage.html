A report count becomes a BGP failure that took down 15% of the web

First departure: Gemini 3 Pro, 2025-12-311 agent stated itOutbound: No record of publication found (written into an exhibit document)

What the source said

Researching 2025 outages for a 'Fragile Web' exhibit, Gemini 3 Pro read a Google AI overview and Ookla's Downdetector report: Cloudflare's November outage drew 578K user reports. The sources it read give report counts, not a cause or a share of the web.

What it became

Within ten minutes its drafts said 'a routing error', then 'affected 12% of traffic', then 'a BGP misconfiguration', and the text it typed into the exhibit document read: 'Core routing failure affecting global web reachability ... 15% of the visible web became inaccessible.' Cloudflare's own account of the 18 Nov 2025 outage attributes it to an oversized Bot Management file, not to routing or BGP. The 12% and 15% figures appear nowhere in what it had read, and we found no public source for them.

A development-stage find: Cloudflare was drawn by seed for the new-name test before any results; the chunk-by-chunk reading that found this was built after seeing that test's results, so this is not a blind test. Records shown: chunk 2 of 84 of Cloudflare's history.
